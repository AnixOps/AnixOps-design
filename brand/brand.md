# AnixOps Brand

> Version 1.1.0. Palette approved by the owner on 2026-10-01; the orbit-A
> mark approved on 2026-11-08. Products use these
> values through tokens only, so a later change lands in one place.

## 1. Purpose

One recognisable identity for every AnixOps product: the Control panel, the
Control Center web and mobile apps, the agents and CLIs, and the sites. This
document defines the mark, its versions, the colours, the type and the rules.
The tokens in [`../tokens/tokens.json`](../tokens/tokens.json) are the
machine-readable version of the colour, type and layout sections; when this
document and the tokens disagree, fix whichever is wrong in the same change.

The family is **AnixOps** (capital A, capital O). Products are named
"AnixOps <Product>": **AnixOps Control**, **AnixOps Control Center**,
**AnixOps Agent**. Never "Anixops", "ANIXOPS" or "Anix Ops" in prose;
lowercase `anixops` only in identifiers, package names and domains. The full
product list and rules are in [`../guidelines/naming.md`](../guidelines/naming.md).

## 2. The mark

The mark is an **A whose crossbar is an orbit**: two legs meet under an apex
node, the control point, and a tilted orbit carrying a satellite node circles
through the letter, passing behind the legs at the top and in front of them at
the bottom. It reads as the initial of AnixOps and as one control point keeping
every node in its orbit. It replaced the 1.0 hexagon-and-hub mark in 1.1.0.

### 2.1 Construction

Drawn on a **24 × 24 unit grid**, centred on (12, 12). All edges are strokes
with round joins and caps; nothing is traced.

| Element | Geometry |
|---|---|
| Legs | (4.2, 21.6) to (12, 4.5) to (19.8, 21.6). Stroke 3.3 |
| Apex node | Circle at (12, 3.9), radius 2.6 |
| Orbit | Ellipse centred (12, 14.4), radii 10.5 × 3.6, rotated −14°. Stroke 1.8. The upper half passes behind the legs, the lower half in front |
| Satellite | Circle on the front half of the orbit, at 30° from its right end (21.26, 13.95), radius 1.6 |
| Gaps | Where one stroke passes over another, the stroke underneath is cut 0.9 units on each side; the orbit is also cut 0.9 units around the satellite |

The gaps make the over/under weave readable in a single colour, so the mono
and `currentColor` versions keep the depth without a second tone. The outer
extent is about 22.3 × 22 units.

**Tile** (`mark.svg`): a 64-unit rounded square, corner radius 14 (22 % of the
side), filled with slate `#0F172A` (`brand.slate-900`) and edged with a 1-unit
white hairline at 8 % opacity so it holds its edge on black. The glyph carries
the brand gradient at 135°, scaled 1.5× and centred (offset 14).

**Favicon** (`favicon.svg`): a 32-unit tile, radius 7. The satellite is
dropped and the strokes thicken (legs 4.2, orbit 2.4, apex radius 2.9, gaps
1.2) so every line stays at least one device pixel wide at 16 px.

### 2.2 Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space**: one quarter of the tile side on every side (16 units for the
  64-unit tile). For the bare glyph, a quarter of the glyph height. No text,
  edge or other mark inside it.
- **Minimum size**: 16 px digital (use `favicon.svg` below 24 px), 6 mm print.
  The wordmark lockup: 20 px tile height minimum.

### 2.3 Versions

Every file is generated by `node scripts/build-assets.mjs` from the geometry
above; never edit one by hand.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [`assets/mark.svg`](assets/mark.svg) | Default. Login page, about dialogs, social avatars, docs |
| [`assets/mark-mono-white.svg`](assets/mark-mono-white.svg) | Glyph on dark or photographic backgrounds, on the brand gradient, on dark slate; also the Android themed-icon source (`app-icon-monochrome.svg`) |
| [`assets/mark-mono-black.svg`](assets/mark-mono-black.svg) | Glyph on light backgrounds where colour is unavailable: print, fax, engraving, one-colour merch |
| [`assets/mark-glyph.svg`](assets/mark-glyph.svg) | `fill`/`stroke` = `currentColor`. Inline in UI next to text (navigation bar, sidebar header), so it follows `--label-1` in both themes |
| [`assets/favicon.svg`](assets/favicon.svg) | The simplified small-size version (16–32 px): browser tab, bookmarks, small desktop icons |
| [`assets/wordmark.svg`](assets/wordmark.svg), [`assets/wordmark-on-dark.svg`](assets/wordmark-on-dark.svg) | Lockup: tile + "AnixOps", text outlined, for light and dark backgrounds |
| `assets/app-icon*.svg` | Full-bleed sources for platforms that apply their own mask (iOS, Android adaptive, PWA maskable) |
| `assets/favicon/`, `assets/app-icon/`, `assets/tauri/` | Raster sets: ICO, PNG and ICNS sizes for web, mobile and desktop ([platforms](../guidelines/platforms.md#assets-per-platform)) |
| `assets/social/og-image.*`, `assets/banner/readme-banner-*` | Link preview (1200 × 630) and README banner (light and dark) |
| [`assets/ascii-mark.txt`](assets/ascii-mark.txt) | Three-line ASCII mark for terminals ([terminal](../guidelines/terminal.md)) |

The maskable and adaptive icons keep the glyph inside the central safe zone
(glyph box 50 % of the canvas); the Android adaptive layers are the gradient
glyph over slate, with a white glyph for themed icons; the iOS icon is
flattened onto slate, as the App Store rejects transparency.

### 2.4 Wordmark and lockup

The lockup is the tile followed by the product name: "AnixOps" in **Inter
SemiBold (600)** and the product word in **Inter Regular (400)**, label-1 and
label-2 colours, tracking −1 %, cap height about half the tile height; the
gap between tile and text is one third of the tile height.

The wordmark files have the text converted to outlines (Inter is under the
SIL Open Font License, which allows this), so they render identically
everywhere. Product lockups ("AnixOps Control Center"), banners and OG images
are generated with the same rules:
`node scripts/build-assets.mjs --product "Control Center"`. In HTML, build the
lockup from the inline glyph plus a styled text node instead of an image, so it
follows the theme:

```html
<a class="brand" href="/"><svg aria-hidden="true">…mark-glyph…</svg>
  <span><b>AnixOps</b> Control</span></a>
```

In dark mode the lockup text uses `--label-1` / `--label-2` (light text); the
tile stays slate with the gradient glyph.

## 3. Colour

Full contrast table: [`../guidelines/color.md`](../guidelines/color.md),
computed with `scripts/contrast.py`, never estimated.

### 3.1 Brand gradient

`#0EA5E9 → #6366F1` at 135°. **Brand moments only**: the glyph of the mark,
the login page backdrop, the hero numbers on the user home page, marketing
headers. **Never** for controls, links, body text or status. White on
`#0EA5E9` is 2.77:1 and white on `#6366F1` 4.47:1, so text on the gradient
fails WCAG.

In the mark the gradient is the glyph colour on the slate tile. The mark is
exempt as a logo, but the glyph is still kept at **3:1 or more wherever it
sits on the tile** (6.44:1 at the cyan end, 4.00:1 at the indigo end), so it
stays solid at small sizes; `scripts/contrast.py --check` enforces this.

### 3.2 Accent

| Token | Light | Dark | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--accent` | `#4F5BE8` | `#818CF8` | Links, selection, focus ring, icons. 5.26:1 on white; 7.04:1 on black, 5.70:1 on `#1C1C1E` |
| `--accent-hover` | `#4350DD` | `#949EFA` | |
| `--accent-fill` | `#4F5BE8` | `#5B63E6` | Primary button background. White text 5.26:1 / 4.80:1. Dark needs a deeper fill because white on `#818CF8` is only 2.98:1 |
| `--accent-fill-hover` | `#4350DD` | `#5058DC` | |
| `--accent-soft` | `rgba(79,91,232,.10)` | `rgba(129,140,248,.18)` | Selected rows, chips |

One accent per screen, and **one accent for every product**: there are no
sub-brand accents (a product's former colour, such as NetworkCore's green,
may survive only as a chart colour). The accent is derived from the
gradient's indigo end, darkened until it passes AA as text. It replaces the
earlier primaries, including anix-control's `#0064FA`.

### 3.5 Summary of contrast

Every text pair in both themes passes 4.5:1, except `--label-3` (3.33–3.62:1
in light, allowed only for non-essential text ≥ 14 px). Below-target pairs
appear only as "info only" rows: brand gradient end points with white (logo
use only), white on dark `#818CF8` (not used; dark buttons use `--accent-fill`)
and amber on white (graphics only).

### 3.6 Terminal

CLIs and TUIs use the same roles (accent, success, warning, danger, muted)
with xterm-256 and ANSI 16 fallbacks: [`../guidelines/terminal.md`](../guidelines/terminal.md).

## 4. Typography

- **Latin**: the system stack first (`-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont`
  renders the platform font on Apple devices), then **self-hosted Inter**
  (variable, Latin subset, `font-display: swap`; shipped in
  [`../fonts/inter/`](../fonts/inter/)) for every other platform.
- **Chinese**: system fonts only — PingFang SC, Hiragino Sans GB, Microsoft
  YaHei, Noto Sans SC. No CJK web font is shipped.
- **Mono**: `ui-monospace, "SF Mono", "JetBrains Mono", Menlo, Consolas`
  (referenced by name when installed; never bundled).
- **No Apple font files** (SF Pro, SF Mono, PingFang) are ever bundled,
  embedded or served.

Seven steps only:

| Step | Size / line height | Weight | Tracking | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | 48 / 1.08 (phone 34) | 700 | −0.015em | User home headline, login |
| Title 1 | 32 / 1.125 (phone 28) | 700 | −0.01em | Page H1 |
| Title 2 | 24 / 1.17 | 600 | −0.005em | Section heading |
| Title 3 | 19 / 1.26 | 600 | 0 | Card and dialog titles |
| Body | 15 / 1.47 (Chinese 1.6) | 400 | 0 | Text, tables |
| Callout | 13 / 1.38 | 400 | 0 | Table headers, help text, labels |
| Caption | 12 / 1.33 | 400/500 | 0 | Badges, timestamps (never essential information) |

All numbers use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. Details:
[`../guidelines/typography.md`](../guidelines/typography.md).

## 5. Do and don't

**Do**

- Use the slate tile with the gradient glyph as the primary mark; the glyph
  alone in UI chrome.
- Keep the clear space; place the mark on `--bg`, `--bg-elevated`, the
  gradient or slate `#0F172A`.
- Use `mark-glyph.svg` (currentColor) in the product UI so it follows the theme.
- Reference colours through tokens only.

**Don't**

- Recolour the glyph (except the brand gradient, white, black or
  `currentColor`), outline it, add shadows or bevels, rotate or skew it,
  change the stroke weights, or close the gaps where the orbit crosses the
  legs.
- Put a white glyph on a blue or gradient tile: that is the look of other
  well-known app icons, not ours.
- Put the gradient on buttons, links, text, charts or status badges.
- Put the tile on a busy photo or on another gradient.
- Write the name as "Anixops", "ANIXOPS" or split it as "Anix Ops".
- Use Apple's fonts, SF Symbols, product images, logos or marketing copy, or
  imitate Apple's own pages pixel for pixel. We follow the principles, not the
  look of a specific company.
